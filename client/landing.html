How it works

Three moves. One flywheel.

OHM proved the model: a treasury that only grows, a token backed by what it holds, and stakers paid in the protocol's own supply. eOHM brings it to the chain where the traders already are.

01
STAKE

Compound every epoch

Stake eOHM, receive seOHM. Every five minutes the index steps up and your seOHM balance grows with it. Unstake any time after the lock; payouts go out from the treasury.

02
BOND

Buy eOHM below market

Pay HYPE, WHYPE or USDC to the treasury and receive eOHM at a 5–8% discount, vesting linearly over five days. Claim to your wallet or claim & stake in one click.

03
BACK

A treasury you can read

Every bond lands in one wallet on Elysium. Its HYPE and USDC balances are read from the chain every 30 seconds and shown live, so backing is a number anyone can check.

The (3,3) loop

Bonds fill the treasury. Stakers hold.

If everyone stakes, everyone compounds. If someone bonds, the treasury grows and backing rises for every holder. Selling is the only move that breaks the loop, and the game is built so it pays least.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is eOHM?

eOHM is an OHM-style reserve protocol on Elysium, the Hyperliquid L2 built by Kinetiq. Holders stake eOHM for seOHM, which grows every epoch as the index rebases. Bonds sell eOHM below market for HYPE, WHYPE or USDC, and every payment goes into the protocol treasury.

What is (3,3)?

It is the game-theory shorthand from OlympusDAO. If two players both stake, both earn (+3, +3). If one sells, the seller gains less and the stayer is hurt. The protocol pays the most to the people who stay staked, and bonds route new capital into the treasury instead of onto the market.

How do bonds work?

Pick a market (HYPE, WHYPE or USDC), enter an amount and sign the payment in your wallet. It goes straight to the treasury on Elysium. The eOHM server reads the transaction receipt from the Elysium RPC, checks that it came from your wallet, went to the treasury and succeeded, then credits the amount that actually arrived. Each transaction can be credited once. Your eOHM vests linearly over 5 days.

How is the bond price set?

Bond price = eOHM market price × (1 − discount). The USD value of a HYPE or WHYPE bond uses the live HYPE mid price from Hyperliquid, refreshed every 30 seconds. USDC is valued at $1.

When can I stake?

Staking accepts eOHM, so it opens the moment the $eOHM token launches on Elysium. Until then, bond to get eOHM below market. When it vests you can claim & stake in one step.

Where does the APY come from?

From emissions. Each epoch the index increases and every seOHM balance grows by the same rate. It is paid in eOHM, so its dollar value depends on the eOHM price and on the treasury that backs it. The app shows the current rate, the next rebase and the runway at current emissions.

Who holds the treasury?

The treasury is a single wallet on Elysium, 0x6A690F711928E8b938Fb5FE38F6fc2B8164Abc97. Its key is held offline by the operator and never touches the server. Bond payments and staked eOHM sit there, and its HYPE and USDC balances are read live and shown on the dashboard.

How do unstaking and payouts work?

Unstaking (after the lock) and claiming vested eOHM to your wallet both join the payout queue, and the treasury sends the tokens on Elysium. Claim & stake skips the queue and moves vested eOHM straight into seOHM.

Which wallet and network do I need?

Any EVM wallet such as MetaMask or Rabby. The app adds Elysium for you (chain ID 99901, RPC rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz, gas in HYPE). You need a little HYPE for gas, bridged from Hyperliquid through Kinetiq.

What are the risks?

eOHM's price can fall. APY is an emission rate, not a guaranteed return. The treasury is held in one wallet, rebases and vesting run in the eOHM ledger rather than a contract, and payouts are sent by the operator. Only bond or stake what you are prepared to lose.
